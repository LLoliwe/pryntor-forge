Require trusted manifest/expected hash/source revision and clear distinction between validated fixture and independently approved provider evidence.

Correctly blocked: single positive charge among 100, unauthorized write tool name, NaN charge, string 'true' permission, non-synthetic fixture. These are useful offline field validations. They are not verified vendor billing settings, permission enforcement at execution, or live API safety.
